Dora Jar

For Dora Jar's music video, I worked as an assistant to both the art director and set builder, collaborating closely with production designer Dale Oliver Slater and director Jocelyn Anquetil. The role was hands-on — helping construct and dress the set, making sure every detail lined up with the overall creative vision. It was a really valuable experience to be that embedded in the process, seeing how a concept moves from idea to something physical and fully realised on camera. Working at that intersection of set design and art direction gave me a much more grounded understanding of how visual storytelling comes together in a production context.
